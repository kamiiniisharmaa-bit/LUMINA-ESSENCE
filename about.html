The Lumina origin

Lumina Essence was born from a simple belief: objects we use daily should carry warmth, soul, and purpose. Founded in 2018 by designer Elena Marchetti, our studio began in a small atelier in the Pacific Northwest, blending ancient craft techniques with modern, eco-conscious design.

We partner with master artisans across the globe — from ceramicists in Mexico to weavers in India — each piece bearing the unique fingerprint of its maker. Every candle, vase, and textile is created with slow, intentional methods: low-waste production, natural pigments, and renewable materials.

“LUMINA” means light — the guiding force behind our designs. We create objects that illuminate spaces and rituals, inviting quiet presence into everyday life.

What we stand for ✦
