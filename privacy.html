Privacy Policy

Your privacy is a cornerstone of our artisan philosophy. At Lumina Essence, we are committed to protecting your personal data with the same care we put into our handcrafted goods.

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our customers. The types of information we gather include:

Identity Data

Name, email address, phone number, billing/shipping address, and account credentials when you register or make a purchase.

Transaction Data

Payment details, order history, wishlist items, and returns/exchanges information.

Technical Data

IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and how you interact with our website (via cookies).

Communication Data

Messages you send to our support team, newsletter preferences, and product reviews.

2. How We Use Your Information

Lumina Essence uses your personal data for legitimate business purposes, always respecting your rights. These include:

  • Order fulfillment: Processing payments, shipping products, and providing order updates.
  • Customer support: Responding to inquiries, warranty claims, and resolving issues.
  • Personalization: Recommending products that match your preferences and improving your shopping experience.
  • Marketing (with consent): Sending you emails about new collections, artisan stories, and exclusive offers. You may unsubscribe at any time.
  • Legal compliance: Detecting fraud, preventing abuse, and complying with applicable laws.
Your trust matters: We never sell your personal information to third parties. Any data sharing is limited to essential services (payment processors, shipping carriers) bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality, analyze site traffic, and remember your preferences. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Essential cookies (required for checkout, security) cannot be disabled. For analytics, we use privacy-respecting tools that anonymize IP addresses.

Types of cookies we use: session cookies (temporary), persistent cookies (remember login), and third-party cookies from our payment gateway. By continuing to use our site, you consent to cookie usage as described.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. For example:

  • Order data is kept for 7 years to comply with tax and warranty obligations.
  • Account information is retained until you request account deletion.
  • Marketing preferences are stored until you unsubscribe or request removal.

After the retention period, your data is securely anonymized or deleted from our systems.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including GDPR for EU residents and CCPA for California consumers), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict processing: Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Opt out of direct marketing or profiling.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@luminaessence.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • PCI-DSS compliance for payment card processing.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability scanning.
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.

While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, we continuously update our safeguards to protect your data.

7. Children's Privacy

Lumina Essence does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16. Our products are intended for adults and individuals of legal purchasing age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately so we can delete the information.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (e.g., social media platforms, payment partners). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. International Data Transfers

Lumina Essence is based in the United States. If you access our website from outside the US, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US where our servers and central database are located. By using our services, you consent to this transfer. We ensure that appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for data transfers from the EU/UK.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Material changes will be notified via email or a banner on our website.

Questions or concerns? Our dedicated privacy team is here to help. Reach us at privacy@luminaessence.com or mail: 417 Harmony Lane, Portland, OR 97201, USA. We take every concern seriously.
